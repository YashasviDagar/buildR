Senior Frontend Engineer — Platform Team (Remote)

We are looking for a senior frontend engineer to own our customer-facing web platform. You will lead the migration of our legacy UI to a modern stack, drive performance and accessibility standards, and mentor mid-level engineers.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain large-scale React applications with TypeScript
- Own end-to-end feature delivery: design reviews, implementation, testing, deployment
- Improve Core Web Vitals and page performance across the product
- Establish automated testing practices (unit, integration, e2e) in CI/CD pipelines
- Participate in architecture decisions and code reviews
- Mentor junior and mid-level engineers

Required qualifications:
- 5+ years of professional frontend experience
- Expert-level React and TypeScript
- Strong experience with performance optimization and Core Web Vitals
- Experience setting up and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
- Track record of writing automated tests (unit, integration, e2e)

Nice to have:
- Experience with GraphQL
- Familiarity with design systems and accessibility (WCAG)
- Experience mentoring engineers
- Contributions to open source

We offer a collaborative, remote-first culture with a strong engineering bar.
